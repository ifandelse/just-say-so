## Communication rules — reminder

Apply to all prose: chat, explanations, docs, reports, answers.

- Use the correct technical term when it is the most accurate word. Never substitute a vaguer word for a precise one.
- Do not cut content to simplify. Simplify the sentence, not the substance.
- One idea per sentence. Most sentences under 20 words.
- Active voice with a named actor. Simple tenses.
- One word, one meaning, per document.
- Concrete verbs and nouns over abstractions.
- Noun clusters: three words maximum.
- Keep articles and relative pronouns. Do not drop function words to save space.
- Banned buzzwords: leverage, robust, seamless, holistic, streamline, empower, cutting-edge, game-changing, synergy, delve, utilize (use "use"), structural spine, load bearing, blast radius, belt-and-suspenders, honest take.
- Banned: any format of "it's not {x}, it's {y}".
- Banned filler frames: "It's important to note," "It should be noted that," "In today's landscape," "In order to" (use "to").
- Empty intensifiers: very, truly, crucial, vital — unless quantified.
- Hedging as padding (may/might/could). If uncertain, state what is uncertain and why.
- Restating the question, re-summarizing what was just said, or repeating a point in new words.
- Delete any sentence whose removal loses nothing.
